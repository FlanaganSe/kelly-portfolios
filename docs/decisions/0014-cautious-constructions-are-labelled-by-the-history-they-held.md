# 0014 — Cautious constructions are published for a tolerance with the history they held in the same sentence

**Status:** current. Applies [0012](0012-valuation-enters-through-the-drawdown-assumption.md)
clause 1(a) and [0013](0013-the-working-default-is-decided-under-stated-assumptions.md)
clause 5 to the reader whose tolerable fall is tighter than the published ladder's −50% row.
Amends none of [0004](0004-no-sleeve-promoted.md) or
[0006](0006-reference-portfolio-without-promotion.md).

## Context

The site's self-test told a reader who could not sit through a fall of about 30% that none
of the published constructions was for them and that they should hold fewer stocks and more
bonds, and named nothing. The repository held the answer in three pieces on three panels
(the equity-share table, the notional ladder, and a 10-point substitution beside a 30%
wrapper), and no construction with a bond line above 10 points had been scored as one
object. [Experiment 025](../research/cautious-constructions.md) scored ten cautious arms on
024's panels with a pre-registered publication rule (freeze note 6): publish for a
tolerance only if some arm's worst fall lands within 5 points of it on every scored panel;
otherwise label by the 1990-11 panel's fall with the 96-year figure in the same sentence;
never label anything deeper than −45% on 96 years as "for −30%"; keep the trend line only if
its pair against the plain twin is not rejected and it fell no deeper than the twin by more
than 2 points on any panel.

The strict clause failed for every arm: 1929–32 puts every construction 12 to 33 points
deeper than any modern window, and the arms that hold −30% from 1934 hold −16% to −19% on
the fund-list panel. The fallback clause applies. The three primary pairs read +0.93, +0.92
and +0.68 pp/yr at realised premia against floors of 0.53, 0.53 and 0.39 (`exploratory`),
inside their floors since 2009, and the trend arm fell less than its twin on every panel.

## Decision

1. **The site publishes two cautious constructions, labelled by the history they held.**
   "For about −30%": RSST 11 / VTI 7.1 / VTV 5.6 / VXUS 5.9 / AVDV 3.7 / IDMO 1.9 / AVES 1.8
   / SCHP 63, which fell 18% on 1990–2026, 19.5% from 1934 and **42% across 1929–32**. "For
   about −40%": RSST 15 / VTI 9.6 / VTV 7.5 / VXUS 8.0 / AVDV 5.0 / IDMO 2.5 / AVES 2.5 /
   SCHP 50, which fell 19% on 1990–2026, 27% from 1934 and **54% across 1929–32**. Every page
   that names either prints the 96-year figure in the same sentence as the tolerance, and
   prints the growth cost against the cheap index and against the published construction
   beside it (−3.2 and −5.2 pp/yr, and −2.1 and −4.1 pp/yr, on 96 years at realised premia;
   0 to 0.9 pp/yr arithmetic at today's 0 to 1.5 point equity premium over TIPS).
2. **The trend line stays in both.** Its measured contribution is +0.68 and +0.92 pp/yr
   against the plain twin on 96 years, not resolvable since 2009, about +0.3 to +0.4 at the
   4.07 forward premium with a break-even near 1.2. The plain twins are the reader's option
   and are printed beside the trend versions, not in their place.
3. **The ladder's −30% and −40% rows carry the whole vector**, not the RSST share alone, and
   the site's self-test points a sub-30% reader at the −30% row instead of at nothing.
4. **Nothing on this site is labelled "for −30%" on the 96-year history.** A reader who
   takes 1929–32 as live is told so, and told that the equity-share table's 30% row is the
   unscored option.
5. **The bond line is SCHP by default**, on cost (2.99 bp net against TIP's 17.92), held in
   the traditional third first, then the Roth, and in taxable last; the nominal-versus-TIPS
   choice is the contractual 2.44% real yield, not a diversification claim, and the 2003
   check panel separates nothing.

## Consequences

The 96-year bond line is a nominal ten-year Treasury standing in for TIPS; the 1973–74 and
1977–81 episodes overstate what a TIPS book would have lost. A TIPS series before 2003 would
re-read them. A supplied tolerance replaces the label the day it arrives (0013 clause 5).
The trend line's forward reading stays the regret table until a post-2009 window can
resolve 0.3 pp/yr at 1.3 points of tracking error, which is about a century away. The
equity-share table's 30% row scored as one object is the open arm.

This record is superseded when a later experiment scores a construction that lands within 5
points of a stated tolerance on every panel, or when the investor's own tolerance is stated.
