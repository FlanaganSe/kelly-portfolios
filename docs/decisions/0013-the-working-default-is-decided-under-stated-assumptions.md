# 0013 — The working default is decided under stated assumptions, and the tolerances are review triggers

**Status:** current. Applies [0012](0012-valuation-enters-through-the-drawdown-assumption.md)
to the reference investor and closes the September 2026 discovery round. Amends none of
[0004](0004-no-sleeve-promoted.md), [0006](0006-reference-portfolio-without-promotion.md) or
[0010](0010-bars-carry-a-reopening-condition.md).

## Context

The investor's brief asks for the most confident portfolio, says the August 2026 valuation
must count, accepts leverage with a purpose, and has never supplied the two inputs the
construction's remaining choices turn on: a maximum tolerable drawdown and a tracking-error
tolerance against a cap-weighted global index. On 2026-09-02 eleven agents ran Experiments
019 to 023, three exploratory studies, a web discovery sweep and an adversarial review. The
results are in the research pages; what matters for this record is that none of them moved a
line of the published vector, that the adversarial review found the trend band resting on a
retracted premium and the "contributions carry the position" argument unsupported, and that
the one valuation rule with a positive record now has a registered result
([Experiment 022](../research/valuation-and-the-allocation.md)).

A recommendation that ends in "supply the tolerance" is not a decision. Every portfolio is
conditional on a risk tolerance; a manager who lacks the number states an assumption, decides,
and names what would change it.

## Decision

1. **The working default for the reference investor is** RSST 25 / VTI 19 / VTV 15 /
   VXUS 16 / AVDV 10 / IDMO 5 / AVES 5 / ten-year TIPS 5, with the TIPS in the traditional
   third beside the wrapper and no taxable line moved
   ([part A](../research/portfolio-for-one-investor.md) §7). The assumption it rests on is
   stated: an investor who names the valuation concern and accepts leverage sits between the
   drawdown ladder's −50% and −60% rows. It is the 25% wrapper every route admits after the
   holdability correction, and the vector the excess-CAPE-yield rule produces in that third.
   Scored as one object ([Experiment 024](../research/portfolio-for-one-investor.md)), it
   trails the published vector by 0.64 pp/yr [0.40, 0.87] on 1929–2025 at the panel's
   realised premia, by 0 to 0.2 pp/yr of log growth at the forward premia, and wins only if
   the equity premium over bonds is about 1.5 pp or less; it buys 1.8 points of maximum
   drawdown. The default is therefore a stated bet on the valuation reading, priced, and not
   a free lunch.
2. **The published construction stays** RSST 30 / VTI 19 / VTV 15 / VXUS 16 / AVDV 10 /
   IDMO 5 / AVES 5, which is the same book at a tolerance of −70% or looser, and the site
   shows the ladder beside it. The two vectors are within resolution of each other on every
   measurement the repository holds.
3. **The international share moves to 60/40 by contributions and stops there** unless the
   investor states a tracking-error tolerance above about 1% a year, in which case the regret
   grid ([valuation](../research/valuation-and-the-allocation.md) §5.7) supports 55/45 at 98
   bp and 50/50 at 152 bp. 40/60 is not supported.
4. **Nothing measured in the September round is added.** Cross-asset carry (Experiment 019),
   a regime-conditioned Treasury stack (020), leveraged 200-day rules and the UPRO/TMF mix
   (021), a financed gold-and-bitcoin stack, and intangible-adjusted value each carry a
   reopening condition on its own page; none is a standing ban.
5. **A stated tolerance is a review trigger, not an open question.** When the investor
   supplies a drawdown tolerance, the ladder's row replaces clause 1 the same day: −50% or
   tighter moves to the 19.1% wrapper with 10 points of TIPS, −60% to 23.7%, −70% or looser
   to the published 30%. A tracking-error tolerance does the same for clause 3.

## Consequences

The recommendation and part A state a decided vector, the assumption under it, and the
trigger that replaces the assumption. Dated triggers: RSST's 2026-07-31 Form N-PORT and
JPFP's first, due by 2026-09-29, refresh the wrapper's delivered loading; RSSY's 36th filed
month in 2027-06 reopens carry. Open research designs that could change a line need data the
repository does not hold: a TIPS series before 2003, a point-in-time term-premium series
before 1990, and a costed cross-asset carry series.

This record is superseded when the investor's own inputs replace its assumptions, or when a
later record admits a new line on registered evidence.
