## Context

Decision 0014 publishes SCHP 50 / RSST 15 / stocks 35 for a tolerance of about −40%, with
its plain twin printed beside it as the reader's option. Half of that book is a ten-year
Treasury line, and its worst measured episode against the published 30% construction is
1977–81 (+36% against +86%). Every trend result in this repository funded trend from stocks;
none had funded an engine from the bond line, whose expected excess is 1.6 pp/yr realised
and 0.8 forward rather than equity's 7.75.

Experiment 026 scored nine such constructions as whole objects against the published
cautious portfolio on 025's panels, with the decision rule frozen first: replace the vector
only if the pair is `exploratory` on both panels, no deeper on any read window by more than
2 points, not below the reference in 1977–81 or 2022 Jan–Sep, *and* resolvable at the
repository's central forward premium; offer it as a printed option if all but the last hold;
otherwise 0014 stands.

Trend stacked on the bond line (RSBT-like, bond kept) read +0.61 / +1.23 / +1.84 pp/yr at
10 / 20 / 30 points against floors of 0.35 / 0.71 / 1.06 on 1929–2025 and +0.99 / +1.97 /
+2.95 against 0.61 / 1.20 / 1.79 on 1990–2026, `exploratory` on both; fell 1.4 to 6.1 points
less on the two full panels and within 0.3 points from 1934; added 9 to 28 points across
1977–81. Trend sold from the bond line read +0.48 / +0.96 / +1.44 and +0.74 / +1.48 / +2.22,
also `exploratory` on both, 0.4 to 1.1 points deeper from 1934. Both were inside their floors
at the 4.07 pp/yr central forward premium (+0.30 / +0.60 / +0.90 stacked, in the fixed
ratio 0.85 to the floor) and near zero since 2009. Gold stacked on the stock line was
`unresolved` at every size on both panels and worse in 2022.

## Proposed decision

1. **The published cautious vector does not change.** Freeze note 6 (a4) failed for every
   arm: the forward gap at the central premium is inside the arm's own floor, in a ratio
   that no size can move. The vector-changing reading requires a gross forward trend
   premium above about 4.6 pp/yr, which the repository's central estimate does not supply.
2. **The RSBT-stacked line at 10 points is printed beside the cautious vector as the
   reader's option**, in the same manner and with the same standing as the plain twin from
   0014 clause 2: SCHP 40 / RSBT 10 / RSST 15 / stocks 35, with its 96-year fall (−52.6%
   against −54.0%), its from-1934 fall (−26.9% against −26.8%), its measured gap and floor,
   and its regret row (+0.30 pp/yr at 4.07, break-even 1.07, inside a 0.35 floor) in the same
   sentence. Ten points rather than twenty or thirty because the resolution ratio is the same
   at every size while gross notional, a third wrapper's fee, its closure risk and its
   ordinary-income distribution rise with size.
3. **The sold-from-bonds line is described, not printed as an option.** It is the one
   substitution in this programme that reads positive, and its 1977–81 and 2022 figures are
   the best in the table, but its break-even depends on an unlevered fund's delivered
   loading (1.60 at full loading, 2.39 at DBMF's measured 0.671) and it is deeper from 1934.
   A reader who wants it is pointed at the synthesis.
4. **Gold on the stock line is not offered.** `unresolved` at every size, worse in 2022,
   deeper on its own window, and priced at a gold premium the repository does not hold.
5. **Nothing here is a claim about the funds.** The option is a construction on public
   series; RSBT's live record since 2023-02 is printed wherever the option is.

## Reopening conditions

- A registered forward trend premium above about 4.6 pp/yr gross moves the stacked line
  from clause 2 (option) to a vector change at any size; below about 1.1 it removes the option.
- A delivered trend loading above 0.9 at 85 bp or less for an unlevered fund brings the
  sold line's break-even to the stacked line's and reopens clause 3.
- A gold forward premium estimate, or a gold series before 1968, reopens clause 4.
- A TIPS series before 2003 re-reads every 1970s figure in this record, for the cautious
  portfolio and for every arm alike.
- The investor's own tolerance, when supplied, replaces every label here (0013 clause 5).

## Consequences if adopted

The cautious page gains one option row and loses nothing. No figure record, notional
budget or sizing calculation changes. The programme's funding-rule lesson gains its first
measurement from the bond side: stacking beats selling by the term premium, and both beat
the equity-funded substitution the programme has rejected before. The trend line's forward
reading remains the regret table, as it is for the RSST line.
