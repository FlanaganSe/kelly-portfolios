# The numbers the site prints

**Question.** For each portfolio the site shows, at exactly the weights it shows, what did
$10,000 become, how fast did it grow, how far did it fall and for how long, and what did it
do in the years a reader will ask about?

**Decision it informs.** Which number a portfolio page or card may print. Every headline
figure on a portfolio page comes from the two files this page describes, never from a
research table whose arm held different weights.

**Out of scope.** Whether any portfolio beats another: the gaps, intervals and statuses
live in the experiment syntheses ([final construction](final-construction-test.md),
[cautious constructions](cautious-constructions.md),
[decision 0013](../decisions/0013-the-working-default-is-decided-under-stated-assumptions.md)).
This page is descriptive and carries no status.

`as of 2026-09-04`. Generated by
[`site_series.py`](../../research/src/portfolio_edge/reporting/site_series.py) into
[`src/content/series/portfolios-1990.json`](../../src/content/series/portfolios-1990.json) and
[`portfolios-1929.json`](../../src/content/series/portfolios-1929.json); ledger run
`b6d5d763…` (family `site_series`, no specification hash: a re-emission of scored arms, not a
trial). The tables below are transcribed from those files and the files are the record.

## 1. What these numbers are, and are not

**Everything here is simulated from index data. No fund's price record is used.**

- On the 1990 to 2026 history each fund is 016f's basis expression: the factor series it
  loads on, less its expense ratio and a trading charge, rebalanced once a year. RSST is
  1.072 × US market plus 1.0 × AQR's TSMOM index, financed on the futures leg, charged 99
  bp; **the TSMOM index is before trading costs**, and RSST's own record starts in
  September 2023. VT has no basis expression and is the 65/35 VTI/VXUS cheap control.
- On the 1929 to 2025 history every stock fund is the US market at 3 bp, so the one-fund
  portfolio and the plain stock index are the same line and the value lean is not shown;
  the trend part is the repository's own four-market trend rule, before trading costs;
  rebalanced monthly.
- On both histories **the bond part is a modelled nominal ten-year Treasury standing in for
  TIPS**, because no inflation-protected series exists before 2003
  ([cautious constructions](cautious-constructions.md) §1, freeze note 3).
- Dollars are nominal, before tax, after modelled fund costs.

Each file carries a `basis` paragraph saying this in plain English. A page that prints a
dollar figure from these files prints that paragraph, or a sentence from it, beside the
number.

## 5. Definitions

- **$10,000 became**: the last value of a path that starts at $10,000 the month before the
  window opens and compounds every monthly return. **Growth a year** is the compound rate
  over the whole window, two decimals.
- **Worst fall**: the deepest peak-to-trough fall of that path. **Months, peak to
  recovery** counts from the peak month to the first month back at or above it, so
  October 2007 to January 2013 is 63. The experiment tables print a different count,
  "months under water", which is the *longest* run of months strictly below any prior
  peak; for the stock index that run is March 2000 to July 2005 (63 months, a coincidence
  with the 2007 count), and for the cautious portfolio it is December 2021 to March 2024
  (26 months) while its deepest fall was 2008. The two counts can differ by one on the
  same episode (185 against 184 for the 1929 fall).
- **Calendar years** are compounded from the twelve monthly returns; a year the window
  does not hold in full is omitted. **Best** and **worst year** are read from those.
- **Since 2009** restarts the path at $10,000 in January 2009.
- Dollars are rounded to whole dollars, percents to one decimal, growth a year to two.

## 6. Provenance and how to regenerate

The emitter loads Experiment 025's pinned sources and both of its panels through 025's own
functions (`load_series`, `build_legs`, `build_primary_panel`, `load_tournament_inputs`),
translates each printed vector into the panel's tickers (VT to the 65/35 control; SCHP to
the modelled ten-year `TSY10`; on the 1929 panel every stock fund to `CORE` and RSST to
`RSST_LIKE`), simulates with `constant_weight_path` (annual rebalancing, 1990) and
`simulate_arm` (monthly, 1929), and before writing anything re-simulates twelve research
arms and compares their worst fall and months under water with the committed tables in
[`00c0b8b0…/tables.md`](../../research/artifacts/00c0b8b0b1894993afdc07236e402451/tables.md)
and [`5f3c2db9…/tables.md`](../../research/artifacts/5f3c2db962fe420881d3aaba3e44df55/tables.md).
All twelve matched to the printed precision on 2026-09-04 (market −52.69 (63), cautious
−18.08 (26) and −15.84 (31), RSST 25 + ten-year 5 −47.38 (42) on the 1990 panel; market
−83.67 (184), cautious −53.77 (74) and −41.70 (69), working default −81.00 (163) on the
1929 panel). A mismatch stops the run.

The cautious arm the artifacts scored held VTI 9.6 and RSST 14.9; the site prints VTI 9.5
and RSST 15 so the vector sums to 100, and the tables above are for the printed vector
(−18.1% against the arm's −18.08%; on the 1929 panel −54.0% against −53.77%).

```sh
cd research && uv run python -m portfolio_edge.reporting.site_series
```

Each file's `provenance` block lists the experiments (016e, 016f, 024, 025 on the 1990
panel; 018, 024, 025 on the 1929 panel), the three artifact run ids, both specification
hashes and the pinned data-manifest hashes. `research/tests/unit/test_reporting_site_series.py`
checks the summary arithmetic by hand, the committed files' shape, and that the emitter's
check constants are what the committed tables say; `src/lib/series.test.ts` checks that
the weights in `src/content/portfolios.ts` equal the weights the emitter scored.
