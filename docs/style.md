# Register

What the site may claim, and how it may sound. This is the one part of a superseded
refactor brief worth keeping: it is enforced nowhere in tooling, and a dozen `fix(copy)`
commits exist because it was breached. `AGENTS.md` names the register; this page supplies
the vocabulary.

## Claims

The site may discuss portfolios **designed** to beat the market. It may never say one
will.

| Use | Never |
| --- | --- |
| Designed to outperform | Guaranteed to beat the market |
| A higher-conviction approach to long-term outperformance | This portfolio will always outperform |
| The strongest candidates the research identified | Proven winning strategy |
| Why this portfolio may outperform | Risk-free alpha |
| The evidence for the strategy, and what would cause it to fail | The perfect portfolio |
| Expected sources of excess return | — |

Confident, skeptical, direct, evidence-driven. A finding travels with its status, its
`as of` date, its interval and its source, or it does not ship
([decision 0007](decisions/0007-application-may-render-research.md)).

## Prose

Write for a sophisticated non-professional investor. Avoid: *in today's rapidly evolving
landscape*; *unlock the power of*; *delve into*; *navigating the complexities of*;
*game-changing*; *revolutionary*; *seamlessly*; *robust* where a precise word exists;
repetitive three-item rhetorical lists; empty closers such as *ultimately, the best
portfolio depends on your goals*; repeated disclaimers; a *Key Takeaways* heading on every
page; describing every strategy as *powerful*; paragraphs that restate their own heading;
fake quotations; and unsupported numerical precision.

The difference, on one subject:

> Momentum has a strong historical record, but it is not a free return premium. It can
> reverse sharply after market rebounds, and weak fund design can lose much of the
> theoretical edge through turnover.

not

> Momentum investing is a powerful and dynamic strategy that empowers investors to
> capitalize on prevailing market trends while navigating an ever-changing landscape.

After drafting a page, edit once with no goal but removing filler, repetition and
inflated claims.
