**Caveats.**
- EXPLORATORY. Decision 0002 stands: nothing here may promote a sleeve and nothing here may appear in the app as a finding. A change of comparator cannot lift that ceiling.
- EVERY basis is fitted IN SAMPLE. A richer basis is a HARDER test of the product, not a fairer one in the investor's favour, and no fitted weight vector here describes a portfolio anybody could have held.
- This experiment cannot make a product better or worse. It says only how much of a published number was a property of the thing it was compared against.
- The set of funds excluded from their own basis GROWS with the basis, and for those funds the shortfall is the realised style return of 2020-2025 rather than an implementation cost. A status change on such a fund is a change in what is being measured.
- The placebo bases add columns without adding a size-by-style cell. They are the floor under any claim that an expressive basis corrected something, and they must be read beside the expressive bases rather than after them.
- No new alpha test is performed and no alpha is re-corrected. Every alpha, MDE, loading and interval is Experiment 013's, reproduced.
- Item B.5 returns remain fund-reported, unaudited and uncorroborated by any independent source, in every basis alike.
- Every figure is PRETAX.
