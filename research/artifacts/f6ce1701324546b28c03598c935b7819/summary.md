### Ex-US factor product exposure and implementation audit

- **Status:** `exploratory` — a search, not a finding; it may not be quoted as evidence that anything works
- **Run:** `f6ce1701324546b28c03598c935b7819`
- **Experiment family:** `exp_009_exus_factor_products`
- **Specification hash:** `e99e2a6e27fbf301a351ed7863820a3f5fb56e99a7183f30321444721d899687`
- **Run kind:** `exploratory`
- **Evidence class:** `fund-implementation-audit`
- **Falsifier:** A fund is rejected as an implementation candidate when ANY of: (a) the point estimate of its loading on its intended factor, in its own region's FF5+UMD panel, is below 0.15 over its own common period; (b) that loading's sign flips between the two fixed calendar halves, evaluated only where the fund's filed history covers both; (c) its tracking difference against the in-sample long-only combination of cheap broad ex-US funds that best replicates it is worse than its fee premium over that combination by more than 0.50 percentage points per year; or (d) its total realised cost of ownership, being its expense ratio plus any tracking-difference shortfall, exceeds 1.0 percentage point per year above the regional broad-market comparator without a corresponding exposure. A fund is `unresolved` when no clause fires but the 95% bootstrap interval on its intended loading contains 0.15, which is what a short window is expected to produce. A t-statistic on residual alpha is NOT a falsifier in either direction. SHORT HISTORY IS A FINDING: a fund whose filed history cannot detect its own intended loading is reported with its sample length and its minimum detectable effect and marked `unresolved`, never rejected for the shortness of a window it did not choose. Under no outcome may this specification produce a status above `exploratory`; decision record 0002 fixes that ceiling for all fund-level work and the window fixes it again.

Screened 537 region-and-factor matching series from the union of the 2019q4 and 2025q4 N-PORT censuses; 26 passed the predeclared ex-US screen and 25 had at least 36 filed monthly returns. 12 product(s) reached `exploratory`, 8 were `rejected` on the frozen falsifier and 5 are `unresolved`. Median usable history is 76 months against Experiment 002's uniform 72. The binding constraint is the data contract and the length of the available windows, not the evidence.

Gross, net-optimistic and net-pessimistic are separate columns and are never collapsed; the spread between them is the cost-model uncertainty.

**Caveats.**
- EXPLORATORY. Decision 0002 stands: this may not promote a sleeve and may not appear in the app as a finding.
- Windows run from 27 to 78 months and are UNEQUAL across funds, so a cross-fund comparison of alphas is a comparison of differently powered estimates. The median minimum detectable alpha at 80% power is 3.23 pp/yr, larger than any plausible true alpha.
- A fund with fewer than 36 usable months is reported and excluded from estimation rather than estimated badly. Its absence is a statement about its age, not about the product.
- Every return here is NET OF FOREIGN DIVIDEND WITHHOLDING, which is deducted before net asset value is struck and is not recoverable inside the fund. Form N-PORT carries no foreign-tax-paid figure, so the charge is bounded by the pedestal comparison and never measured. A taxable US shareholder may recover part of it through the foreign tax credit; a retirement-account shareholder may not. Neither case is modelled.
- The tracking difference is against a CONSTRUCTED benchmark -- the regional comparator fund and an in-sample fitted combination of cheap ex-US funds -- and never against the fund's own stated index, because index levels are licensed and no free source with a documented contract carries them.
- The replicating combination is fitted in sample. An investor could not have known those weights in advance, so it is a best case for the replication and the comparison against it is deliberately hard on the product.
- The French international files are built from a different vintage than the US file, their second moments were never gated against any printed table, and their dividend-tax treatment is undocumented. Every ex-US loading inherits that, and it is why the regional validation gates are looser than the US ones.
- Item B.5 returns are fund-reported and unaudited, and General Instruction G lets each filer use its own methodology.
- Public N-PORT filings begin in 2019, so no census here can see a fund that closed before then. The measured attrition is a LOWER BOUND on survivorship contamination even after renames are separated from deaths.
- 25 funds cleared every screen and had usable returns; 8 were rejected on the frozen falsifier, which is a statement about delivered exposure and cost, not about whether the underlying factor exists.
